Frequently asked questions

No. They only see your number and your message, like any normal chat.

Does this work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. The wa.me link works on Android, iPhone, WhatsApp Web and WhatsApp Desktop.

No. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is sent to a server.
